## Review scope

Every source received metadata and hash inspection plus distributed precise-seek visual samples. Candidate sequences received denser frame review and short playback review. This is a sampled review of the long raw recordings, not a claim that every frame of more than an hour of source footage was manually inspected.

The final selected exports were fully decoded. Combat selections were checked for black/frozen spans and visually reviewed in output contact sheets. The main reel was assembled from the five reviewed sequences, re-encoded to 1280×720, 30fps H.264/yuv420p and fully decoded. Its duration is 72 seconds. Three focused clips are 24, 24 and 15 seconds. Original sources contain audio; public edits are deliberately silent. No generated gameplay, added performance claim or release status is implied.

OS titlebars/taskbars were removed only from selected windowed recordings. Source aspect ratios are retained through letterboxing; gameplay HUD, prototype state and technical diagnostics remain visible. No color grading changes the recorded game. Original sources stay untouched on F:.

## Website QA

Videos use native controls, playsinline and preload=none. Focused demonstrations open through native details and never autoplay. Tests cover primary playback, focused playback, closing/visibility pause, BFCache recovery, no-JavaScript captions/controls and mobile layout. The main page uses actual development stills with accurate captions. Per-source hashes, exact crops, output hashes and encoding are recorded in the adjacent environment/combat/projectiles/showcase manifests.
